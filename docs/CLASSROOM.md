# One-hour lesson plan: verify a program

For a class, club or youth group of 6 to 30 students. No coding needed. Students leave with a real contribution they can put on a resume.

**Goal:** each pair verifies one program card against its official page and reports what they found.

| Min | Activity |
|---|---|
| 0 to 5 | Show the Programs page. Ask: "Would you trust this? What would make you trust it?" Introduce the rule: no official page, no card. |
| 5 to 10 | Read `docs/VERIFYING.md` together. Model one check on the projector with a real card. |
| 10 to 40 | Pairs pick a card in their own town. They open the official page and fill out the seven facts. They note anything different on the card. |
| 40 to 50 | Pairs post what they found. Anything wrong goes in the [report form](https://github.com/VadneyK/internships/issues/new?template=update-program.yml). Students under 13 give their findings to the teacher, who files them. |
| 50 to 60 | Reflect: What surprised you? Which programs would you actually apply to? Who could you tell about this site? Add a resume line (see `CONTRIBUTORS.md`). |

**Materials:** a projector, laptops or phones, a printout of the verification checklist.

**Safety:** GitHub accounts require age 13+. Keep all discussion in public issues. The teacher or youth leader should read each report before it is filed. Do not post student names, schools or schedules.
