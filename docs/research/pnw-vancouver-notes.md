## Tips for a teen in this region

- Many hospital volunteer programs here take 16 year olds but keep patient-care roles for 18 and up. Providence Health Care in Vancouver lets 16 and 17 year olds help only at its long-term care sites. Source: https://www.providencehealthcare.org/en/get-involved/volunteer
- BC Children's Hospital sends recruitment information three times a year (for January, May and September starts), so apply early. Source: https://app.betterimpact.com/PublicOrganization/77a715dc-d284-4b7b-8590-6782245d8250/1
- Harborview requires full shot records with the application and denies incomplete ones, so gather them before you apply. Source: https://www.uwmedicine.org/volunteering/harborview
- Point Defiance Zoo Guide applications open in December, months before summer. Source: https://www.pdza.org/connect/volunteer/
