## Tips for teens in Silicon Valley (each with a source)
1. The Tech Interactive's teen Holiday Helper application is only open Oct 9 to Oct 23, 2026. https://www.thetech.org/support-us/volunteer
2. The best paid summer job for juniors and seniors is KP LAUNCH: $25 an hour, apply Nov 16, 2026 to Jan 8, 2027. https://kplaunch.kaiserpermanente.org/high-school-program/
3. Stanford SIMR opens Dec 18, 2026 and closes in late February 2027. It strongly favors local Bay Area students. https://simr.stanford.edu/
4. Stanford AIMI Summer Research Internship opens Nov 10, 2026 and closes Feb 1, 2027. https://aimi.stanford.edu/education/summer-research-internship
5. Stanford's MEDCSI costs thousands of dollars ($6,580 for the 2026 on-campus option). It is a paid class, not an internship. Free Stanford options include SMYSP (juniors from low-income, first-generation families) and PIPS. https://med.stanford.edu/medcsi.html
6. NASA's main internships are for college students. High schoolers should look at NASA GeneLab for High Schools (free, online, U.S. citizens and permanent residents). https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/internship-programs/
7. San Jose teens ages 14 to 18 who face barriers to work can apply year-round for San Jose Works. https://www.sanjoseca.gov/your-government/departments-offices/office-of-the-city-manager/economic-development/san-jos-works/faq
8. The County of Santa Clara Student Intern Level I (high school) has no open posting now. Subscribe to the job alert or email departments. https://esa.santaclaracounty.gov/career-seekers/student-internship-program-information-students
9. Hospital volunteering often has long waits. Washington Health (Fremont) says the next high school round is Fall 2026. El Camino Health asks you to check mid-January 2027. https://www.washingtonhealth.com/volunteering/ and https://elcaminohealth.org/node/121266
10. Stanford Health Care says volunteering does not count as shadowing or clinical hours. https://stanfordhealthcare.org/for-patients-visitors/volunteering.html
11. Foothill College waives enrollment fees for high school dual enrollment up to 11 units a term. You still buy books. https://foothill.edu/hs/
12. Many local high schools say you should finish 10th grade before taking community college classes. https://mvhs.fuhsd.org/guidance-student-support/high-school-planning/enrichment-opportunities
13. Under 18 means you need a work permit for paid jobs. In FUHSD, the student, employer and parent all sign. https://www.fuhsd.org/students/work-permits
14. Mountain View and Palo Alto youth councils recruit once a year: Mountain View in February, Palo Alto in summer. https://www.mountainview.gov/our-city/city-council/boards-commissions-and-committees/youth-advisory-committee and https://www.mitchellparkteenservices.org/payc
15. Valley Water's Youth Commission (ages 13 to 18, Santa Clara County high schoolers) opens again in Spring 2027. https://www.valleywater.org/learning-center/valley-water-youth-commission
16. Palo Alto Art Center counselor-in-training registration opens Feb 4, 2027. https://www.paloalto.gov/Departments/Community-Services/Teen-Services/ACT
17. Hiller Aviation Museum takes Flight Teen volunteers (ages 14 to 18) all year. https://hiller.org/volunteer/
18. Redwood City's Leaders in Training program costs families $49 to $98 per session, so it is not free. https://www.redwoodcity.org/departments/parks-recreation-and-community-services/teens/leadership-in-training-l-i-t
19. Some websites list "Youth At Work" and the Mountain View library site mvpl.org. Both domains now go to for-sale pages. Do not use them.

## Warnings
- Pay-to-play: Stanford MEDCSI ($6,580 on campus in 2026), Stanford CARE Explorers ($5,500 in 2026), Stanford Pre-Collegiate Summer Institutes (tuition not read), Redwood City LIT ($49 to $98), SJSU summer courses (tuition). None are jobs or guaranteed internships.
- Outside lists that say high schoolers can do NASA OSTEM internships conflict with NASA's own page, which says college only.
