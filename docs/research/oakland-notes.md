## Tips for a teen in this region
1. Oakland's summer jobs run through partner nonprofits, not the City. Pick a partner and apply to them. Source: https://www.oaklandca.gov/Government/Working-for-Oakland/Summer-Jobs-and-Internships-for-Youth/Youth-Employment-and-Training
2. The City says MSYEP jobs are free to join and pay at least Oakland's minimum wage for up to about 100 hours. Same source as tip 1.
3. The City Council approved more than $3.1 million for 2026 and 2027 summer youth jobs, so expect a 2027 round. Source: https://www.oaklandca.gov/News-Releases/City-Council-Advances-Major-Investment-in-Summer-Opportunities-for-Oakland-Youth
4. Berkeley YouthWorks: the Winter 2027 interest form is due October 30, 2026, and you must fill out a new form each time you apply. Source: https://berkeleyca.gov/youthworks
5. Berkeley Public Library says YouthWorks accepts job applications in March and October only. Source: https://www.berkeleypubliclibrary.org/teens/jobs-and-volunteering
6. Oakland's City recreation jobs (lifeguard, recreation leader) have an Oct 25, 2026 deadline for Spring/Summer 2027, then Feb 7, 2027. Hiring takes months. Source: https://www.oaklandca.gov/topics/parks-recreation
7. EBMUD and BART internships take no direct applications. A partner group or school must refer or sponsor you. EBMUD partners: ACE Mentor, Biotech Partners, Eden Area ROP, Girls Inc., Youth UpRising. Sources: https://www.ebmud.com/jobs/internship-and-training-programs and https://bart.gov/node/23341
8. BART high school interns earned $20 an hour in 2026. Source: https://bart.gov/node/23341
9. Berkeley Lab Experiences in Research pays $500 a week; the 2026 window ran Feb 2 to Mar 22. Start your application in early February. Source: https://k12education.lbl.gov/programs/high-school/experiences-in-research
10. Lawrence Hall of Science Teen Internship pays $3,500 for the summer; the 2026 deadline was January 15. Source: https://lawrencehallofscience.org/job-postings/teen-internship-2024/
11. Oakland Zoo teen programs charge small fees ($50 to start, aid available) and have spring and fall windows. The paid ZooCamp interns earn $400 a week. Source: https://www.oaklandzoo.org/support/volunteer/zoocamp-teen-assistants-interns
12. Start as an unpaid ZooCamp Teen Assistant to qualify later for the paid intern roles. Same source as tip 11.
13. Peralta Colleges dual enrollment: high schoolers pay no tuition or fees for up to 15 units a semester (CCAP students). Start with your counselor. Source: https://www.peralta.edu/dual-enrollment/students
14. OUSD students: the Linked Learning office handles work permits and internship placements. Source: https://www.ousd.org/linkedlearning
15. Hidden Genius (Black male students, rising grades 9 to 11) and SMASH (grades 10 to 11, 3.0 GPA) are free and apply in winter; both pages we read showed old dates, so check for new ones. Sources: https://www.hiddengeniusproject.org/immersion and https://www.smash.org/programs
16. Richmond residents ages 16 to 21 can apply online for a paid 100 hour internship with Richmond YouthWORKS. Source: https://richmondca.gov/1139/Work-Experience-Program
17. Alameda's Youth Career and Job Fair is a spring event (April 1 in 2026) for Alameda high schoolers. Source: https://www.alamedaca.gov/Shortcut-Content/Events-Activities/Youth-Career-Job-Fair-2026
18. Oakland library teen volunteers earn service hours, and two thirds of its library volunteers are teens. Source: https://oaklandlibrary.org/teenvolunteers
19. USS Hornet in Alameda takes volunteers 16 and up for restoration and visitor services (16 to 17 need adult supervision). Source: https://uss-hornet.org/volunteer
20. Habitat for Humanity East Bay/Silicon Valley takes volunteers 16 and up with a signed minor waiver. Source: https://www.habitatebsv.org/volunteer
