## Tips for a teen in this region
1. Temecula's next free teen service morning is Nov 23, 2026, at the Mission Hope food pantry, for ages 14 to 18. Sign up before you go, because there are only 20 spots. Source: https://temeculaca.gov/Volunteer
2. Mt. San Jacinto College takes Spring 2027 class requests only until Nov 13, 2026. Source: https://msjc.edu/apply/high-school-students.html
3. College of the Desert has three dates for free classes: Nov 6, 2026 for winter, Jan 29, 2027 for spring, and Mar 5, 2027 for summer. Source: https://www.collegeofthedesert.edu/students/free-early-college/default.php
4. Crafton Hills dual enrollment students register during open registration, Nov 20, 2026 to Jan 18, 2027 for spring. Your parent and your school each get 7 days to sign, so start early. Source: https://www.craftonhills.edu/admissions-and-records/dates-and-deadlines/priority-registration-dates-spring.php
5. San Bernardino Valley College forms for spring were due Oct 23, 2026. The college says late forms do not promise a seat, so ask if you missed it. Source: https://www.valleycollege.edu/academics/specialized-programs/dual-enrollment/enrollment-process.php
6. Norco College's spring deadline for all steps is Feb 6, 2027, and the spring term starts Feb 16, 2027. Source: https://www.norcocollege.edu/academicaffairs/ep/hse/documents/nc-dual-and-concurrent-enrollment-deadlines-2026-2027.pdf
7. GPA rules differ by college for classes you take on your own. Riverside City College asks for a 3.0, and Norco College asks for a 2.0. Sources: https://www.rcc.edu/become-a-student/dual-enrollment-student.html and https://www.norcocollege.edu/academicaffairs/ep/hse/concurrent.html
8. Fullerton College dual enrollment is for partner schools, and you should talk to your counselor first so you get on your school's list. Source: https://dualenrollment.fullcoll.edu/apply/
9. San Diego City College high school registration for spring 2027 starts Dec 1, 2026. Source: https://www.sdcity.edu/students/concurrent.aspx
10. At San Diego City, Mesa and Miramar colleges you must send the high school form again each term. Source: https://www.sdccd.edu/students/new-students/high-school-students.aspx
11. MiraCosta College starts a new Early College Credit form on Nov 6, 2026. Source: https://www.miracosta.edu/admissions-and-aid/high-school-students/concurrent-enrollment/index.html
12. The San Diego Natural History Museum takes juniors and seniors for school credit only. You need to be in an internship class at school, be 16, and have taken one biology course. Source: https://www.volgistics.com/appform/1533533673
13. The VA San Diego summer youth program had a May 15, 2026 deadline and 50 slots, for ages 14 to 17. Watch for it in spring. Source: https://www.va.gov/san-diego-health-care/work-with-us/volunteer-or-donate/
14. Loma Linda University Health takes volunteers from age 16, but if you are 16 or 17 you need a 3.2 GPA. Source: https://lluh.org/patients-visitors/volunteer-services
15. Rep. Torres's Youth Advisory Committee takes high school students in nine cities of the 35th District until Dec 31, 2026, and spots fill as people apply. Source: https://torres.house.gov/services/resources/youth-advisory-committee
16. Many city youth groups pick members in spring or summer. Brea Volunteens closed April 20, 2026, and Cypress closed July 16, 2026, so plan ahead for next year. Sources: https://cityofbrea.gov/volunteens and https://www.cypressca.org/activities/teen-activities/yac
17. La Habra Teen Leaders picked its 2026 group in May, and how many hours you can give is a big part of who gets picked. Source: https://www.lahabraca.gov/930/Teen-Leaders-Program
18. Placentia Library says it is now accepting teen volunteers ages 12 to 17, and you need a library card and a personal email. Source: https://placentialibrarydistrict.ca.gov/teen-services
