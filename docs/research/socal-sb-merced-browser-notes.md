# Southern California, Santa Barbara and Merced: pages read in a browser (checked 2026-10-16 to 2026-10-17)

These notes cover GitHub issues #19 and #27. Both list official pages that blocked our automated reader.

## How this was researched
- Every page was opened in a real browser and read by hand. Search engines were used only to find official pages for leads with no link.
- No CAPTCHA or bot check was solved or worked around. Pages that stayed behind a check are listed under "Still blocked".
- Before adding a card, data/programs/ was searched by name and URL. Nine leads already had cards from earlier batches and were left alone.
- Where 2027 dates are not posted, cards use last year's dates with deadline_confidence last-year-pattern.
