## Surprises
- SF YouthWorks is for grades 11 to 12; MYEEP is for grades 9 to 10 in the 2026-27 round.
- The NIH high school internship is effectively gone; only graduating seniors who are 18 can apply.
- The sf.gov list says Children's Council ECE pre-apprenticeship has a grade 11 to 12 pathway, but the application form says 18 plus. I left it out.
- Hacktoberfest 2026 no longer counts pull requests toward rewards.

## Tips for a San Francisco teen (each with a source)
1. SF YouthWorks (grades 11 to 12) and MYEEP (grades 9 to 10) both closed Sept 14, 2026 for the school year. Summer 2027 for YouthWorks opens in February. https://www.sf.gov/september-2026-dcyf-newsletter
2. MYEEP summer applications are due the first week of April. https://www.ymcasf.org/myeep
3. Opportunities for All takes ages 13 to 24 and the 2026 deadline was April 10. Plan for early April 2027. https://www.sf.gov/hrc-youth-career-exploration-opportunities
4. The Community Youth Center job fair is Oct 14, 2026 at Stonestown, 12 to 4 p.m. (ages 14 to 24). https://www.sf.gov/opportunities-for-youth-ages-14-24
5. The city Mega Job Fair is Oct 22, 2026, ages 16 and up. https://www.sf.gov/opportunities-for-youth-ages-14-24
6. Greenager pays $25 per hour and its 2027 interest form is open now. https://sfrecpark.org/workreation
7. Careers in Science at Cal Academy opens again in February 2027 for rising 9th and 10th graders at SFUSD schools. https://calacademy.org/interns
8. SFPL YELL (paid) opens each March. https://www.sfpl.org/teens/volunteer-job-opportunities
9. The SF Zoo takes teen volunteers from age 12 (Nature Trail) and 15 (Junior Zoologists). https://www.sfzoo.org/volunteer/
10. Mission Bit coding classes are free for ages 14 to 18, and College Connections (grades 9 to 12) is Nov 7, registration due Oct 27. https://www.sf.gov/opportunities-for-youth-ages-14-24
11. The US Senate Page application at Senator Padilla's office is open Oct 13 to Oct 26, 2026 for Spring 2027. https://www.padilla.senate.gov/senate-page-program/
12. The Congressional App Challenge closes Oct 26, 2026. https://www.congressionalappchallenge.us/rules/
13. Regeneron STS (seniors) closes Nov 5, 2026. https://www.societyforscience.org/regeneron-sts/
14. RSI is free and takes juniors until Dec 11, 2026. https://www.cee.org/programs/research-science-institute
15. Berkeley Lab Experiences in Research pays $500 per week; expect a Feb to March 2027 window. https://k12education.lbl.gov/programs/high-school/experiences-in-research
16. National Park Service Youth Conservation Corps (ages 15 to 18) opens in late January 2027. https://www.nps.gov/subjects/youthprograms/ycc.htm
17. UC COSMOS is for California students only, and 2027 applications are "coming soon". Expect January to February. https://jacobs.ucsd.edu/cosmos
18. NASA internships need college enrollment. Use NASA citizen science instead as a teen. https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/internship-programs/ and https://science.nasa.gov/citizen-science/
19. Forage simulations are free, but they are practice, not a job. https://www.theforage.com/
20. The California deadline for the US Senate Youth Program was Oct 7, 2026, set by the California Department of Education, which is earlier than the national Dec 1 date. https://ussenateyouth.org/selection_process_administrators
21. Girls on the Run Bay Area's own page lists an Aug 26, 2026 junior coach deadline while sf.gov lists Oct 12, so email first. https://www.gotrbayarea.org/junior-coach
22. When a page says "snippet-only" in this data set, call or email the program before you plan around it.
