# Pathways research notes (school, college, trades, workforce, volunteer, legal)

Date: 2026-10-07. Scope: quiet school-based and skills-based on-ramps for teens in Davis/Yolo/Sacramento, Silicon Valley/San Mateo/Fremont, and Oakland/Berkeley/East Bay.

- Workforce boards: Sacramento Works (SETA), work2future, NOVAworks, Alameda County WDB, Oakland (Youth Employment Partnership, summer program), Yolo (snippet-only).
- Volunteering: Sacramento Food Bank, Alameda County Community Food Bank, two Habitat affiliates, HandsOn Bay Area, Key Club, four public library systems, UC Davis Health, Humane Society Silicon Valley, Sacramento SPCA.

## Tips for a teen (each with source)
1. The free route to college classes is dual enrollment. Los Rios starts at age 14 or ninth grade with parent and principal OK; tuition is free. https://losrios.edu/admissions/dual-enrollment
2. Woodland CC non-CCAP classes need 8th grade done; free up to 11 units. https://wcc.yccd.edu/enroll/dual-enrollment/
3. Peralta (Laney, Merritt, Berkeley City, Alameda) dual enrollment covers Oakland, Berkeley and Alameda students; fees excused up to 15 units with CCAP. https://merritt.edu/dual-enrollment
4. Silicon Valley juniors and seniors in six districts can take free half-day career classes at SVCTE with a bus ride from their home school. https://svcte.metroed.net/Course-Information
5. Eden Area ROP is for juniors and seniors in Castro Valley, Hayward, San Leandro and San Lorenzo; enroll through your counselor. https://www.edenrop.org/high-school-programs
6. In Yolo County, ask your College and Career Center about B-Unbound: about 60 hours with a mentor and a stipend up to $500. https://www.ycoe.org/College-and-Career-Readiness/Work-Based-Learning/index.html
7. OUSD students: the paid summer internship list is updated weekly January to May. Fill out the common application early. https://www.ousd.org/high-school-linked-learning-office/for-students-families/internships
8. Work Experience Education has three types; the unpaid exploratory type can start at 12 and does not need a work permit. https://www.cde.ca.gov/ci/ct/we/
9. A work permit comes from your school, not the employer. Casual babysitting or lawn mowing in private homes, and true self-employment, do not need one (2013 state guide). https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/ChildLaborLawPamphlet.pdf
10. Hours rule of thumb (2013 guide): ages 14 and 15 can work 3 hours on school days; ages 16 and 17 can work 4. Check dir.ca.gov for updates. https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/ChildLaborLawPamphlet.pdf
11. California defines an apprentice as 16 or older, but most apprenticeship programs want 18. Use the state search to check each one. https://www.dir.ca.gov/das/
12. Police and fire Explorer programs often start at 14 or 15 and end around 20 or 21; apps open at set times (Hayward: January and July; Cosumnes Fire: Spring 2027). https://hayward-ca.gov/police-department/programs/police-explorer-program
13. Santa Clara County Teen CERT takes ages 13 to 18; sign up for the waitlist for the next academy. https://sites.google.com/view/santa-clara-county-teen-cert/home
14. Food banks take young volunteers: Sacramento from age 10 (with an adult under 16); Alameda County from age 10, solo at 15. https://www.sacramentofoodbank.org/volunteer and https://www.accfb.org/volunteer/
15. Habitat build sites need you to be 16. Younger teens help with fundraising or lunch. https://www.habitatebsv.org/volunteer/
16. Animal shelters usually need 18 for hands-on work; teens can foster with an adult or make blankets at home (HSSV, Sacramento SPCA). https://www.hssv.org/volunteer/
17. Libraries are an easy first volunteer role: Oakland (13 to 17), Santa Clara County (14+ tutoring), San Jose (teens 12 to 18). https://oaklandlibrary.org/teens/teen-volunteers/
18. If you earn $400 or more working for yourself, you must file a tax return. https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/self-employed-individuals-tax-center
19. WIOA youth services at Sacramento Works start at 16 and can include paid work experience; call or visit a job center. https://sacworks.seta.net/?p=152
20. Job Corps (ages 16 to 24) is free and live-in; a federal settlement in August 2026 keeps centers open. https://www.deseret.com/education/2026/08/12/99-job-corps-centers-including-clearfields-to-stay-open-as-settlement-is-reached/

## Warnings
- No pay-to-play or "guaranteed internship" brokers were included. Red Cross lifeguard and babysitting classes are fee-based by provider.
- Do not treat EMT, phlebotomy or California Conservation Corps as teen options; all need 18 (see excluded.json).
