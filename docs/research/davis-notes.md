# Davis, Yolo and Sacramento notes (checked 2026-10-07)

## How this was researched
- Official pages were read with a browser for sites that block automated fetches (City of Sacramento, City of Davis, Yolo County, UC Davis). Search engines were used only to find official pages. The shared WebSearch budget ran out mid-task, so the later searches used DuckDuckGo results in the browser.
- 48 of 50 entries were read on an official page. 2 are snippet-only (Sutter Roseville junior volunteer, SMASH at UC Davis).
- Many pages show 2026 dates because the 2027 cycle is not posted yet. Those entries are marked last-year-pattern.

## Gaps not closed

## Tips for teens (each with a source)
1. The Sacramento Zoo's Zoo Teens 2027 applications open Oct 10, 2026 (ages 14 to 19, 2.75 GPA, letter of recommendation). https://www.saczoo.org/volunteer
2. Kaiser Permanente LAUNCH summer 2027 applications open Nov 16, 2026 and close Jan 8, 2027. Use a laptop. https://kplaunch.kaiserpermanente.org/internship-program-updates/
3. COSMOS at UC Davis opens Jan 6, 2027 and closes Feb 5, 2027. Financial aid forms have a hard deadline. https://cosmos-ucop.ucdavis.edu/app/main/page/dates-deadlines
4. Summer @ City Hall (Sacramento) 2027: apply Apr 1 to 12, 2027, current grades 9 to 11, $500 stipend. https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/ypce/youth-civic-engagement/summer-at-city-hall
5. SMUD paid internship: last year it ran Mar 23 to May 1. Selection is a random drawing, so apply even if you doubt your chances. https://sacworks.seta.net/?p=187
6. Landscape and Learning (Sacramento, ages 14 to 17, $16.90 per hour) closes after 350 applications. Be ready the day the window opens. https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/ypce/youth-employment/landscape-and-learning
7. Many City of Sacramento youth programs require living inside city limits, and an address that says Sacramento may not be inside. Check the city's address tool. https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/ypce/youth-employment/prime-time-teen
8. UC Davis VolunTEEN needs vaccine and TB records. Start collecting them months before the May deadline. https://health.ucdavis.edu/volunteer/opportunities/summer-volunteen-program
9. Young Scholars (UC Davis) takes only rising juniors and seniors, and applications run Jan 1 to Mar 1. Two teacher references, one in science. https://education.ucdavis.edu/ysp-application
10. West Sacramento students at Washington USD schools can get paid internships through Home Run if they are 16 by day one. https://www.wshomerun.org/internships
11. Dual enrollment is free at Woodland Community College and a good first step toward college credit. https://wcc.yccd.edu/enroll/dual-enrollment/
12. In Davis, ask the DJUSD Internship Specialist about school-year internships (library, Davis Vanguard, UC Davis Center for Valley Fever, Dignity Health Mercy General). https://www.djusd.net/instruction/programs/CTE/student_c_t_e_opportunities
13. Sacramento Public Library says it takes about a month to process a teen application and 2 to 3 months to place you. Drop-in volunteering is faster. https://www.saclibrary.org/about-us/volunteer-opportunities/volunteer-library
14. The animal shelter in Sacramento takes volunteers from age 12, but 12 to 15 year olds must come with a parent. https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/community-development/animal-care/volunteer/volunteer-opportunities/youth-opportunities
15. LegiSchool at Sacramento State pays a stipend for a four-week online civics program for sophomores to seniors. https://www.csus.edu/center/center-california-studies/legischool-project.html
16. Under 18 and getting a paid job in California usually needs a work permit from your school. The Sacramento Landscape and Learning FAQ describes the permit step. https://www.cityofsacramento.gov/ypce/youth-employment/landscape-and-learning
17. Davis Teen Leadership Council meets Oct to May on 2nd and 4th Tuesdays; the 2026-27 application closed Oct 2. Watch for fall 2027. https://www.cityofdavis.org/city-hall/parks-and-community-services/teens

## Warnings
- Do not pay a broker for a "guaranteed internship." Every entry in this file is run by a school, city, university, hospital or established nonprofit. Pre-college programs with a fee (UC Davis Pre-College, COSMOS, Sac State Summer Youth Academies, Roseville LDP, 4-H, Davis aquatics courses) are marked fee-based.
- Sutter Davis Hospital, the California Raptor Center and the Sacramento Zoo's animal care volunteers do not take high schoolers (see excluded.json).
