## Tips for teens (each with a source)
1. Michigan Math and Science Scholars admits on a rolling basis, so apply soon after the application opens in mid-January. https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/mmss/apply-to-the-mmss-program/guidelines-for-applying/
2. Wolverine Pathways takes applications in 6th and 9th grade only, and the next window opens in December 2026. https://wolverinepathways.umich.edu/application-3/
3. Regions Hospital's summer volunteer program takes applications only from January 1 to April 30. https://www.healthpartners.com/care/hospitals/regions/about/volunteer/volunteer-opportunities/
4. Argonne's free summer camps take only 24 to 30 students and want applications by mid-April. https://www.anl.gov/education/coding-for-science-camp
5. Austin and Travis County teens: the county summer jobs program opens Summer 2027 applications in January. Fill out the interest form now. https://www.traviscountytx.gov/health-human-services/children-and-youth/syep
