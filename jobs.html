Jobs that hire teens

Tap your age. You see real employers that say they hire at that age, with a link to the page that says so. Programs and internships are on the Programs page. This page is for regular jobs.

A second tag shows when an age is for some jobs only or the posting is old.

A listing is not a recommendation, and we did not screen any employer. Check the job post for your store and ask a parent before you apply.

Tap your age to see who hires.

Farm and ranch work has its own federal rules. On a farm or ranch on the Safety page lists them.

Getting there without a car: put the employer address into your city transit agency trip planner, check the last bus time against your shift end, and ask your school office about a youth pass.

Stay safe. Never pay to get a job. Never send your ID to a stranger. Tell an adult before an interview. More on the safety page.

Each line comes from the employer's own page or a government page that we read. Many chains are run by local owners, so the age at your store can be different. Always check the job post for your store. Last read Oct 10, 2026.

Words you will see
Non-farm job
Any job that is not farm work; federal law sets 14 as the minimum age for most of them.
Minimum age
The youngest age an employer says it hires for a job, as stated on that employer's own page.
Hazardous job
A job the Secretary of Labor has declared hazardous; no one under 18 may do the jobs on that list.
