The one thing to know

People beat job sites

A referral is when someone who already works somewhere says, “You should talk to this person.”

In one company's hiring data (one hiring system, mostly office jobs), referrals were under 2% of the applications. But they were about 17% of the people hired. Job sites brought in almost half of all applications and about a quarter of the hires.

That data covers adult jobs. The idea carries over: people say yes to people they know. So your first move is not a website. It is a short message to one adult who already knows you.

Source: Gem 2025 Recruiting Benchmarks, company hiring data through December 2024. For college internships, Handshake reports applications per posting more than doubled from January 2023 to January 2025, so posted openings are crowded.

Under 16?

You do not need a fancy internship. Shadowing a grown-up for an afternoon, volunteering at a library or hospital, and starting a small project all count, and many are open at 13 or 14.

Parents and youth leaders

The fastest help is an introduction: one coworker, neighbor or church member who will take a 15-minute call from a teen. Then read the work permit and pay rules and the scam checklist together.
