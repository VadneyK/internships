Your first paycheck

Your check is smaller than hours times pay rate. This page shows what comes out, which forms to fill out on your first day, and what to do if your pay is wrong. Last read Oct 9, 2026.

Facts, not tax advice. We only show amounts we have a source for. Ask a parent or a tax preparer about your own situation.

This page covers California, Georgia, New York and Illinois. Social Security and Medicare apply to most jobs in every state. The exception is working for your parent's own unincorporated business when you are under 18. In another state the state tax form and minimum wage are different. See the work permit finder for your state's minimum wage and the number to call.
