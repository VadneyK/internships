Ages 12 to 14

Too young for a job? Start here.

Most paid jobs start at 14. Before that, you can build skills, earn from people you know, and get a record that makes your first real job easier.

Last read Oct 9, 2026.

Short answer: at 12 and 13 you can usually babysit, do yard work or chores for neighbors, and deliver newspapers. Federal law allows these under 14. Your state can be stricter (New York says a babysitter must be at least 14), so pick your state below.

Words you will see
Non-farm job
Any job that is not farm work; federal law sets 14 as the minimum age for most of them.
Self-employment
Work you run for yourself, not for an employer; if you earn $400 or more from it after your costs, the IRS says you pay self-employment tax.
Joint account
A bank account with two or more names on it. Everyone named on it can usually withdraw money.
