Ages 12 to 14

Too young for a job? Start here.

Most paid jobs start at 14. Before that, you can build skills, earn from people you know, and get a record that makes your first real job easier. Show this page to a parent. Last read Oct 9, 2026.

This is a summary, not legal advice. A blank cell means the state page we read does not say. It does not mean allowed, and it does not mean banned. Ask the number in the last row before you start.
