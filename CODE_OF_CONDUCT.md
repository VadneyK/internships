# Code of Conduct

This project helps teenagers find real opportunities, and many contributors are teenagers. We keep it safe, kind and useful.

## Our promise
- Be kind and patient. Disagree about ideas, not about people.
- Assume good intent. Everyone here is learning.
- No harassment, bullying, hate, or sexual content. Ever.
- Credit other people's work.

## Keeping young people safe
- **Talk in public.** Questions, feedback and reviews happen in public issues and pull requests, where other people, including parents and teachers, can see them.
- **No moving to private chat.** Adults must not ask a minor to talk privately (direct messages, text, social media, video call) about this project. If an adult does, do not reply and report it.
- **No personal information.** Do not post your phone number, home address, school schedule, or photos of other young people. Use a username you would be happy to show a future boss.
- **Parents and guardians are always welcome** to read along, comment, and ask questions.
- Maintainers will not ask for private details about a contributor's family, school or home.

## Reporting
Use GitHub's **Report content** option on any comment, issue or pull request, or open a private report from the **Security** tab of the repository.
If you are under 18, tell a parent or another adult you trust too. Maintainers will remove content that breaks these rules and may block accounts that keep breaking them.

## Scope
This applies to every space for the project: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any place where someone speaks for the project.

This policy is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), with added rules for working with young contributors.
