Papers for your job

Do I need a permit?

Pick your state, your age and the kind of work. You get a plain answer, the permit if you need one, who gives it, and what to bring. Then get a checklist you can print or a message you can send to your school.

Free. No sign-up, no ads. Made by a volunteer, not by Ignition, Acts 2 Network, AYM or a church. Questions: write to us on GitHub.

1. Tell us about the work

We have full answers for 17 states and British Columbia, and shorter answers for 33 more states and Washington, DC. Pick “Another state” to find yours. If it is not there, pick “My state is not listed” to see who to ask.

This is a summary, not legal advice. When the pages we read do not say, the answer is “check before you start” with who to call. Schools and employers can change their forms, so use the links to confirm.

Most pages last read Oct 9, 2026. The California card was read Oct 7, 2026.

Words you will see
Non-farm job
Any job that is not farm work; federal law sets 14 as the minimum age for most of them.
Hazardous job
A job the Secretary of Labor has declared hazardous; no one under 18 may do the jobs on that list.
Withhold
Your employer holds back part of each check to pay tax.
W-4
The federal form that tells your employer how much income tax to hold back from each check.

Pick all three to see your answer.
