Papers for your job

Do I need a permit?

Pick your state, your age and the kind of work. You get a plain answer, the exact permit if you need one, who gives it to you, what to bring, and a checklist you can print or a message you can send to your school. Most pages last read Oct 9, 2026. The California card was read Oct 7, 2026.

This is a summary, not legal advice. When the pages we read do not say, the answer is “check before you start” with who to call. Schools and employers can change their forms, so use the links to confirm.
