Safe at work

Most jobs are fine. This page is for the day something is not: a task that feels unsafe, an injury, or someone who treats you badly. It shows what official pages say, and who to call. Researched in October 2026.

Tell a trusted adult, then use these numbers

      More numbers, including California, Georgia, New York and Illinois, are in Who to call.

      This is a summary of official pages, not legal advice. Each point shows the exact words from the page we read, with a link. Pages change, so open the link before you rely on a deadline or a phone number. We are not a lawyer and cannot tell you what to do about your own job.

      This page covers federal rules plus California, Georgia, New York and Illinois. In another state the rules and the numbers may be different. See the work permit finder for your state, and the number to call.
