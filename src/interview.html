Four things make a first application strong: the days and hours you can work, a few people who already said yes to be a reference, a short answer to “tell me about yourself,” and two questions to ask. Everything you type stays in this browser. Last read Oct 9, 2026.

    Pick the hours you could work each day. We compare them with the hour limits for your age and state. Your availability is not your schedule: an employer can schedule you for less, and the limits apply to the hours you actually work.
