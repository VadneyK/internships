                            How this guide works, and why it is not a chatbot

                            Every page here is fixed text that a person can read, check and print. A teen taps a place or an age and sees the same answer every time, with a link to the official page it came from and the date we read it. Nothing is made up on the spot.

                            What your teen does and does not get

                            • No chat box. Nobody and nothing writes back to your teen.
                            • No account, no sign-in, no email address, no tracking. Choices your teen makes stay in this browser on this device.
                            • No ads and no outside scripts. Links to official pages open in a new tab.
                            • The same answer for every teen who asks the same question, so you can read exactly what yours will read.

                            How the facts got here

                            • Each program, rule and price comes from an official page that was read. When a page does not say, the card says “not stated.”
                            • Computer tools helped find and read pages, and every card was checked again against its own page. What teens see is that checked text, not an answer written live by a program.
                            • Rules and dates change. Each card shows when it was last read and links to the source. If something is wrong, tell us.

                            Why this matters: a live chatbot can sound sure and be wrong, and it can collect what a teen types. A fixed guide cannot do either.
