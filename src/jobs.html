Words you will see
Non-farm job
Any job that is not farm work; federal law sets 14 as the minimum age for most of them.
Minimum age
The youngest age an employer says it hires for a job, as stated on that employer's own page.
Hazardous job
A job the Secretary of Labor has declared hazardous; no one under 18 may do the jobs on that list.
