      Three steps to start

      1. Pick your age and city

        Open Programs. Pick your age and type your city. You see only the programs you can join.

      2. Save the ones you like

        Tap the star on a card to save it to My list. Your list stays on your phone. Nobody else sees it.

      3. Check if you need a work permit

        Under 18 and getting a paid job? Find out in one minute if you need a work permit and how to get one.
