us-atlas 3.0.1, ISC license

The state lines and the country outline in assets/img/us-states.svg are drawn from states-albers-10m.json
in the us-atlas package (https://github.com/topojson/us-atlas), simplified by tools/geo/make_basemap.mjs.
tools/geo/state-check.mjs reads states-10m.json from the same package. The text below is copied from
node_modules/us-atlas/LICENSE.

us-atlas packages the US Census Bureau cartographic boundary files (2017 edition), which are works of the
United States government and in the public domain in the United States.

The build tools d3-geo 3.1.1 (Copyright 2010-2024 Mike Bostock), topojson-client 3.1.0 and topojson-simplify
3.0.3 (Copyright 2012-2019 Michael Bostock) are under the same ISC license text. They run only when the
basemap is rebuilt and are not sent to visitors.
