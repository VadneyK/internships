Where the Map page points come from

City points in data/map.json (made by tools/geo/geocode.py and tools/build_map.mjs)

1. US cities, towns, counties and ZIP code areas: US Census Bureau Gazetteer Files, 2026 edition.
   https://www2.census.gov/geo/docs/maps-data/data/gazetteer/2026_Gazetteer/
   Files used: 2026_Gaz_place_national, 2026_Gaz_cousubs_national, 2026_Gaz_counties_national,
   2026_Gaz_zcta_national and 2026_Gaz_state_national. Each point is the internal point (INTPTLAT, INTPTLONG)
   the Census Bureau lists for that place. These files are works of the United States government and are
   in the public domain in the United States. The raw files are not kept in this repository; see
   tools/geo/README.md for how to download them.

2. British Columbia cities: the Canadian Geographical Names Database, Natural Resources Canada.
   Each point and its source link is in data/geo-overrides.json (manualPoints).
   Contains information licensed under the Open Government Licence - Canada:
   https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada
   (licence named on the download page,
   https://natural-resources.canada.ca/earth-sciences/geography/download-geographical-names-data/9245)

A dot shows the city named on a program's card, not the program's exact address. Every hand fix the
geocoder uses is in data/geo-overrides.json with its reason.

Map lines in assets/img/us-states.svg: see licenses/us-atlas-ISC.txt.

Street map (the drag-and-zoom map on the Map page, assets/js/map-gl.js)

3. Map tiles, assets/map/tiles/base.pmtiles.gz and detail.pmtiles.gz: OpenStreetMap data, © OpenStreetMap
   contributors, through the Protomaps basemap build named in tools/geo/tiles/BUILD
   (https://maps.protomaps.com/builds/). Cut down by tools/geo/tiles/slim.py to the layers the map draws.
   OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License 1.0:
   https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright and https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
   These two files are a derived database of OpenStreetMap and are offered under the same license. They are
   published with the release named map-tiles-<BUILD> in this repository, and tools/geo/tiles/build.sh makes them.
   The tiles' lowest zooms also carry Natural Earth shapes (public domain), and their layer names follow the
   Tilezen schema (MIT, Mapzen), as the Protomaps basemap license explains:
   https://github.com/protomaps/basemaps/blob/main/LICENSE.md

4. Label letters, assets/map/fonts/: Noto Sans glyph ranges from protomaps/basemaps-assets, commit
   028c18f713baecad011301ff7a69acc39bcc2ae7. SIL Open Font License 1.1: licenses/OFL-noto-sans.txt.

5. Map drawing code: MapLibre GL JS 6.11.2 (assets/vendor/maplibre/), licenses/maplibre-gl-BSD-3.txt.
   PMTiles 4.5.0 reader (assets/vendor/pmtiles.js), licenses/pmtiles-BSD-3.txt; it includes fflate, licenses/fflate-MIT.txt.
